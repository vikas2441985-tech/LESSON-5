healthy

Staying healthy gives kids the energy to play, learn, and grow strong through simple daily choices. To fuel your body correctly, eat a colorful mix of fruits and vegetables, drink plenty of water, and limit sugary snacks. Pair this nutrition with at least 60 minutes of active play every day—like riding a bike or dancing—and protect yourself from germs by washing your hands with soap and brushing your teeth. Finally, ensure your body recovers by getting 9 to 11 hours of sleep each night and turning off screens before bed so your brain can fully rest.

quick tips

To easily build healthy habits, try the 5-2-1-0 rule each day by aiming for 5 servings of fruits and vegetables, limiting recreational screen time to 2 hours or less, getting at least 1 hour of active play, and drinking 0 sugary drinks. You can make these habits fun by adding fresh fruit slices to your water bottle, singing a favorite song for 20 seconds while washing your hands, and choosing active, movement-based video games. Finally, try involving kids in meal prep so they are more excited to eat nutritious foods, and swap late-night screen time for a printed book to ensure a restful night's sleep.
